What are the most fun retro OS vibes to recreate?
Gemini
A good retro OS mood usually comes from one strong era, not a mashup of everything.

If you want dashboard energy: Web 2.0 portals, sidebar widgets, glossy panels, and bright accent colors.

If you want messaging energy: compact chat windows, buddy lists, status icons, and message balloons.

If you want assistant energy: Clippy-style help characters, tooltip-style prompts, and a very direct “I’m here to help” feel.

The trick is to pick one dominant metaphor and let everything else support it.
